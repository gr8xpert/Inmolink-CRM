Inmolink v2 — Feature Brief

10 October 2026

Inmolink v2 is one platform where Spanish agencies manage listings and clients, share stock under agreed commission, and power their websites and portals, with AI throughout.

Listings and data

FeatureWhat it does
Agency accounts and verificationSignup, CIF/NIF check, staff approval before sharing or website access
Team and rolesAdmins, agents and developer users; invites, seats, two-factor login
ListingsPer-listing commission, private/shared/public visibility, field locks, price history
Media pipelineEach image stored once on Cloudflare R2 (EU); auto WebP sizes, EXIF removed, HEIC supported
New developmentsDevelopments, phases and units with live availability, versioned price lists, documents
Locations and taxonomy4-level locations with map areas; types and features; feed spelling aliases
ImportsKyero, any XML (visual mapper), CSV/Excel, Resales Online (own listings), Milenio+, Infocasa, Inmoba, thinkSPAIN
Quality score and freshnessFix-it hints per listing; unconfirmed listings hidden from the network after 120 days
BookmarksSaved property folders with notes
Listing overridesAn agency rewrites any network or new-build listing's text for its own channels
Languages9 languages; AI drafts every translation for review
RentalsLong-term rental fields and rental portal feeds

Viewing requests with agreed termsCommission split accepted by both agencies before the viewing
Network boardBuyer requests, offers and distressed sales, auto-matched to members' listings
Chat and notificationsAgent-to-agent chat, in-app and email alerts, WhatsApp handoff
Saved searches and alertsNew listing, price drop, back on market
Deal roomOffer, reservation, arras, completion; documents, handshake, disputes
E-signed agreements and formsCollaboration, reservation and listing agreements with audit trail
Duplicate detectionSame property from several agencies grouped; buyers see one listing
Developer portalDevelopers upload projects, set commission and access, see enquiries
Managed new-build dataAI reads developer price lists, staff approve, prices kept current within 24 h

AI

AI also powers buyer matching, new-build price-list reading, duplicate detection and unique website content above. Prices, commission and legal text always come from data, never from the AI.
